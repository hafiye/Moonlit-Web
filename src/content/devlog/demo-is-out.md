Yayy, I released the demo!

After overthinking what I should present as a demo, I decided to show all the material but end the demo at a certain point. You know, if I gave you 60 pages instead of 400 to tidy up, it would be pretty easy and boring. So there are still 400 pages, but the demo ends after you bind 6 books.

Sooo, technically, after finishing 6 books, you could restart the demo and finish another 6, then another, until you complete all of them... If you’re that enthusiastic, go for it ^^ I’ll be thinking “they must’ve really enjoyed the game! Hehehe” <3

I’m excited to hear what you think, so I’ll appreciate every comment and discussion.

Also, I tried to add as many accessibility options as I could, but testing them was a bit tricky, so if you use any of them, your feedback will be very valuable to me.

glhf <3
